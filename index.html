The Journey

Persistence, mentorship, and a first-generation degree.

I started my internship search as an international student on an F-1 visa, where extra documentation requirements and employer hesitation made an already competitive process harder. Early applications were rough. I didn’t yet know how to present my skills, pitch myself, or use LinkedIn as more than a listing board.

The turning point was becoming a Student Alumni Ambassador in spring 2023. That role put me in rooms with alumni and mentors who helped me rebuild my resume around the right experience, sharpen my elevator pitch, and learn the most underrated skill in a job search: following up with genuine, lasting follow-through. UMW’s Center for Career and Professional Development backed that growth with workshops and one-on-one advising.

That discipline paid off directly: a spring 2025 internship with Banduri and a summer 2025 Business Intelligence internship with Navy Federal Credit Union, both sourced through networks I had to learn how to build. Now, as a Rise Peer Mentor and Resident Assistant, I pay it forward, helping other students, especially fellow international and first-generation students, navigate the same path.

Success is rarely a solo journey. There are people who genuinely want to see you thrive.

01

ProofMode

Live · Mar 2026 – Present

2nd Place · UMW Eagle Egg Pitch Competition

Problem

AI-detection tools guess whether writing "looks like" AI after the fact, an adversarial arms race that punishes honest students and is trivial to spoof.

Approach

Built proof-of-process instead of detection: timestamped writing checkpoints and revision history sealed into tamper-evident PDFs, with field-level encryption, Argon2 password hashing, and signed JWT sessions.

Result

2nd place at the UMW Eagle Egg Pitch Competition; shipped as a live product on FastAPI, Next.js, and PostgreSQL.

AI Use Case for Emergency Alerting Systems

Aug 2025 – Apr 2026

Presented at NCUR 2026 · Richmond, VA

Problem

Emergency alerting regulatory documents are dense and scattered across agencies, making it slow for staff to find grounded, citable answers during time-sensitive decisions.

Approach

Built a retrieval-augmented generation pipeline over the regulatory corpus, Pinecone vector embeddings for retrieval, grounded generation with source citations, exposed through both a Streamlit UI and a CLI.

Result

Selected to present at the National Conference on Undergraduate Research (NCUR) 2026 in Richmond, VA.
